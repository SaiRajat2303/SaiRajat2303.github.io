About Me

I build and reason about the hardware that runs modern compute — from cores and uncore IPs all the way up to data-center fleets. My interests sit at the intersection of computer architecture, memory & coherence, and power/performance modeling for hyperscale systems.

I’ve been lucky to work across the stack: high-performance RISC-V cores and system IP at Tenstorrent, GPU-IP design verification for Pixel-9 at Google Silicon, non-volatile memory IP at Samsung SSIR, and an undergraduate foundation in EEE at BITS Pilani (Hyderabad). This fall I start my PhD at UW–Madison with the HAL Lab.
